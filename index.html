Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my air compressor running constantly while blasting?
Sandblasting is the single most air-intensive workshop tool. A small #4 nozzle (1/8") consumes 21 CFM continuously. If your compressor produces only 12 CFM, your tank pressure will rapidly plummet below the 80 PSI needed for productive blasting.
Can I use beach or playground play sand?
ABSOLUTELY NOT. OSHA strictly prohibits using crystalline silica sand for abrasive blasting. When silica sand impacts metal, it fractures into microscopic respirable crystalline dust that causes irreversible, incurable silicosis lung disease.
Why does moisture in the airline cause blasting to fail?
Compressed air creates significant condensation. Water droplets mixing with fine blast abrasive in the nozzle cause instant caking and mud clogs. A dedicated particulate filter and desiccant air dryer are mandatory.
